# Bober Dam Defense mark

Metaphor: **beaver peeking over a stacked log dam** — night watch on
the river. Horizontal timber bars are the body of the mark. A chunky
beaver head rises above the top log; two giant cream buckteeth hang
over the wood. At 32px the read is **log bars + teeth**, not a face
in a circle, not a slingshot.

Shape: **full-bleed square field** of night purple. Primary silhouette
is a **stack of capsule logs** (rounded ends, staggered, end-grain
circles) filling the lower canvas. Beaver is a **dome / mound** sitting
on the dam — ears as two bumps, no square chocolate-bar portrait, no
iOS squircle tile, no rounded-square + inner glow-circle. Tiny yellow
**crescent moon** in the upper-left sky (dam defense at night).

Palette (4):
- Night purple `#3A2A6A`
- Fur brown `#8B5A2B`
- Bucktooth cream `#F4E6C3`
- Dam wood `#5C3A1A`
- (moon accent) Yellow `#F5C400`

Forbidden (do not reuse):
- **Bober Yeet**: slingshot, sling-V, rubber band, yeet pose, beaver-in-sling,
  square chocolate-bar face as the whole mark
- **Orion / grok-crypto-trader**: constellation, stars, deep-space navy,
  cyan rim, nebula glow, stacked glow-orbs, rounded-square + inner glow-circle
- **Lowlight**: iron lantern, peaked roof, keyhole glass, oxblood / amber lamp
- **ViaPass / VignetteRoute**: cream travel ticket, punch-holes, terracotta
  sun, teal visa stamp
- **ConstructCost**: invoice / calculator tiles
- CMC / MultiversX Bober coin art — do not paste or trace
- Crypto glyphs, wallet, token ticker
- Cyan, nebula, stars-as-constellation, oxblood lantern, terracotta ticket
